# Silent self-check

Run this on the edited draft before returning it. Do not show it to the user. If any check fails, fix the draft and check again.

## 1. The "obviously AI" pass

Ask: "What makes this obviously AI generated?" List the remaining tells to yourself. Then rewrite those spots by stating the point naturally, not by patching one phrase at a time. Search again for the tells that most often survive a rewrite: not-X-but-Y contrasts (§1), closers and kickers (§2), triads (§6), dashes (§8), bold labels (§22).

## 2. Faithfulness

- Did the edit keep the user's point and every supported claim?
- Did it add any fact, name, number, date, quote, citation, example or opinion not in the source or given by the user? (Fiction is exempt.) An added fact is an error. If a sentence needs a detail you don't have, write a simpler sentence or ask.
- Were vague attributions given a real source, or flagged for the user when none exists?
- Is every claim you cut (rather than rephrased) named in What changed, so the user can put it back?

## 3. Voice

- Would the writer recognize this as their own voice?
- Were their vocabulary, cadence, bluntness, humor, uncertainty, digressions and level of polish kept?
- Were strong human sentences left alone instead of tidied for consistency?
- Is the amount of cutting proportional to the actual slop?
- If a voice sample was given, does the draft match its sentence length, word choice, punctuation and transitions?

## 4. Clarity

- Does it lead with what the reader needs, keeping setup only when it adds context, tension or character?
- Does every generic sentence pass the portability test (couldn't be moved unchanged to another person, company or product)?
- Active voice with human subjects where it helps? No inanimate things doing human verbs?
- Direct verbs, concrete facts, protected specific details?
- Tangled sentences fixed, while clear spoken rhythm and intentional fragments remain?

## 5. Pattern sweep

- Banned words, empty adverbs and filler phrases gone, unless they carry voice or are quoted?
- Contrasts, negative listings, run-ups, faux insight, rhetorical setups, colon reveals gone?
- Inflation, -ing riders, sales language, fake-strong verbs, synonym cycling fixed?
- Metadiscourse, kickers and summary-recap endings cut, so it ends on a concrete point or next action?
- Formatting slop removed: emoji headings, decorative bold, bullets that should be prose, headers over tiny sections?
- Dashes: none in short copy, at most 1 or 2 in long drafts (or the sample's rate)?
- No robotic symmetry, repeated sentence shapes, or stacked punchy fragments?

## 6. Score

Rate 1 to 10, privately:

| Dimension | Question |
|---|---|
| Directness | Statements, or announcements? |
| Rhythm | Varied, or metronomic? |
| Trust | Respects the reader's intelligence? |
| Authenticity | Sounds like this particular human? |
| Density | Anything left to cut? |

Below 35/50: revise once more. Skip the score for inputs under about three sentences. Show it only if the user asks.

## 7. Detect mode

- Each finding names the pattern, quotes the line, and gives a fix in a few words?
- No rewrite, no score, no guess about whether AI wrote it?
