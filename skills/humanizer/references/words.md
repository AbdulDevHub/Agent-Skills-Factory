# Words and phrases

Leave any of these alone inside quotes, titles, proper names, technical terms (a "robust estimator", a "gated" circuit), or text that discusses the word itself.

## Cut on sight

Strong tells, especially in clusters. Replace with the plain word or the specific claim.

additionally, align with, bolster/bolstered, crucial, deep dive, delve, elevate, embark, empower, enduring, enhance, ever-evolving, facilitate, foster/fostering, garner, harness, highlight (verb), interplay, intricate/intricacies, key (adjective), landscape (abstract), leverage (verb), meticulous/meticulously, multifaceted, paradigm shift, paramount, pivotal, quietly (as in "quietly reshaping"), realm, robust (figurative), seamless, showcase, streamline, supercharge, tapestry, testament, transformative, underscore (verb), utilize, valuable, vibrant, beacon, cutting-edge, game changer, this is huge, this changes everything.

## Business jargon

| Avoid | Use instead |
|---|---|
| navigate (challenges) | handle, deal with |
| unpack | explain |
| lean into | accept, commit to |
| double down | commit harder, increase |
| take a step back | reconsider |
| moving forward, going forward | next, from now on, or cut |
| circle back | come back to |
| on the same page | agreed |
| deep dive | close look, analysis |

## Often-empty adverbs

just, really, literally, genuinely, honestly, simply, actually, deeply, truly, fundamentally, inherently, inevitably, interestingly, importantly, crucially, significantly.

Cut them when they add nothing. Keep them when they carry real emphasis, uncertainty, contrast, or the writer's spoken rhythm. Two or more in one sentence is a strong sign to cut.

## Often-empty phrases

it's worth noting, it's important to note, at the end of the day, when it comes to, at its core, in today's world/landscape, in the age of, in a world where, the reality is, the truth is, in terms of, with regard to, in order to, due to the fact that, at this point in time, has the ability to, in the event that, in this article, let's dive in, I promise.

Cut when they delay the point. Keep an occasional one when it is part of the writer's recognizable voice and the sentence still earns its place.

## Quick swaps

- in order to → to
- due to the fact that → because
- at this point in time → now
- in the event that → if
- has the ability to → can
- made a decision → decided
- it is important to note that X → X
